Begin their journey with us

Our admissions process is designed to be warm, clear and supportive. We look forward to getting to know your family and helping your child take their first step into a community built on faith, excellence and care.

Kindergarten to Grade 6 is for girls. Our pre-school welcomes both boys and girls from age two.
