Nurturing excellence since our foundation

Immaculate Conception Preparatory School is a Roman Catholic institution dedicated to the holistic formation of young girls. For generations, our doors have welcomed families across Kingston who seek an education rooted in faith, discipline and academic rigour.

We believe every girl is a unique gift, called to discover her God-given potential. Through a nurturing environment and a commitment to excellence, we prepare our students for lives of purpose, integrity and service.

Our pre-school welcomes both boys and girls from age two.

We don't just teach students; we nurture future leaders with a foundation in faith.

Guided by purpose

Our mission and vision shape everything we do, from the classroom to the chapel.

  • Our mission

    Motivated by the teaching of the Gospel and inspired by the example of St. Francis of Assisi, the school community works together as a family, in close association with parents, for the total development of each child.

  • Our vision

    ICPS is committed to providing the right environment to enable children to achieve their full potential.
