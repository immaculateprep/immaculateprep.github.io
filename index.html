A legacy of nurturing excellence

As a pillar of the Kingston community, Immaculate Conception Preparatory School has been dedicated to the holistic development of children. Our mission is to provide an environment where faith and reason go hand in hand.

We believe that every girl is a unique gift, and our role is to help her discover her God-given potential. Through a rigorous academic curriculum and a rich range of extracurricular activities, we prepare our children for the challenges of tomorrow.
