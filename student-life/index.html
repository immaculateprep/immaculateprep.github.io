More than a school, a family

Life at Immaculate Prep is joyful, active and full of opportunity. Beyond the classroom, our children grow through friendship, teamwork and play. From house challenges to Friday assemblies, there is always a way for every child to belong, contribute and shine.

Three houses, one family

Every child belongs to a house, earning points and building lifelong friendships. Our three houses are named after the three places where Our Lady appeared, each carrying forward a legacy of faith, hope and community. Though divided into houses, our students are united as one family, growing together in spirit and sportsmanship throughout the year.

  • Fatima
  • Lourdes
  • Guadalupe

Faith at the heart of everything

As a Roman Catholic school, our faith shapes the rhythm of each day. Morning prayers and Friday assemblies draw our community closer to God and to one another.
