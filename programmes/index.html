A curriculum built for growth

Our academic programme blends rigorous learning with the formation of character and faith. From the early years through to graduation, we nurture curiosity, critical thinking and a lifelong love of learning, so that each girl is well prepared for the next stage of her education.

Our academic levels

  • Ages 2 to 4

    Pre-school

    A joyful, play-based foundation in literacy, numeracy and social skills, rooted in care and Christian values. Open to boys and girls. More about pre-school

  • Ages 3 to 5

    Kindergarten

    Builds a strong foundation for reading, writing and maths while nurturing confidence and curiosity.

  • Grades 1 to 3

    Lower school

    Blends academics with creative and personal development to encourage a lifelong love of learning.

  • Grades 4 to 6

    Upper school

    Balances academic rigour with student leadership opportunities, preparing students for success in high school.
